Privacy Policy — GPS Simulator

Last updated: September 19, 2026

GPS Simulator ("the App") is a developer testing tool that lets a user supply a mock (simulated) location to Android's location system, for software testing and development purposes. This policy explains what data the App accesses, what it sends over the network, and what it never does.

No Account, No Server of Ours

The App has no user accounts, and the developer does not operate any backend server. The App does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs of any kind.

Location Access

The App requests foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to show your real current position on the in-app map, so you can pick a point to simulate or compare it against your simulated position. The App does not request background location permission, but the real-location listener may keep running while the app's process is alive and its screen is bound (for example while the app is open, even if temporarily not on screen), not only at the exact moment the screen is visible. It stops when the app's screen component is destroyed.

Map Tiles and Place Search (Third-Party Servers)

To display the map, the App downloads map tiles from third-party map providers depending on the map style you choose: OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery, OpenTopoMap, or OpenStreetMap France (Humanitarian tiles). Loading a map tile sends the visible map area and your IP address to that provider's servers, the same way any map app or web page with an embedded map would.

If you use the in-app place search, or reverse-geocode a point you tap on the map, the App sends the text you typed or the coordinates you selected to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service, in order to return a place name or address. This is only sent when you actively search or tap to look up a place — not continuously and not in the background.

These providers are independent third parties with their own privacy practices; the App does not control what they log. No account identifiers, contact information, or other personal data beyond what this section describes is sent to them.

Mock Location Data

Coordinates you choose to simulate are used to feed Android's mock location provider (via Developer Options → "Select mock location app") on your own device. This is used only to let other apps on your device see the simulated location while you test them. It is never uploaded or shared with the developer or any third party.

Local Storage on Your Device

The App stores the following locally on your device only — never transmitted to the developer:

  • App settings and preferences (map style, units, etc.)
  • If "Remember last position" is enabled (on by default), the last map centre shown — which may be your real location if you last viewed the map there
  • Your search history, favourites, and saved routes

This data never leaves your device via any developer-operated service, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. You can disable "Remember last position" in Settings if you do not want your last-viewed map position stored.

Permissions Summary

  • Location (foreground only): to show your real position on the map, as described above.
  • Internet: to download map tiles and, when you search or tap a point, to perform place lookups, both described above.
  • Notifications / Foreground service: to show the required "Mock location active" notification while a simulated session is running, and to let you stop it from the notification.

The App does not request background location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or storage permissions.

Children's Privacy

The App is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
