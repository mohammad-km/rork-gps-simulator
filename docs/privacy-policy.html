Privacy Policy — GPS Simulator

Last updated: September 19, 2026

GPS Simulator ("the App") is a developer testing tool that lets a user supply a mock (simulated) location to Android's location system, for software testing and development purposes. This policy explains what data the App accesses and, more importantly, what it does not do.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or to any third party. The App has no backend server and no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.

Location Access

The App requests foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely to:

  • Show your real current position on the in-app map so you can pick a starting point for a simulated session, and
  • Let you compare your real position against the simulated (mock) position while testing.

The App does not request or use background location access. Real location data is read only while the app is open and visible, is used only to render the map on your device, and is never saved, logged, or sent anywhere.

Mock Location Data

Coordinates you choose to simulate are used exclusively to feed Android's mock location provider (via Developer Options → "Select mock location app") on your own device. This data stays on your device and is used only to let other apps on your device see the simulated location while you test them. It is never uploaded or shared.

Local Storage

The App stores your in-app settings (such as map style, units, and the last simulated point) locally on your device only, so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Permissions Summary

  • Location (foreground only): to show your real position on the map, as described above.
  • Notifications: to show the required "Mock location active" foreground-service notification while a simulated session is running, and to let you stop it from the notification.

The App does not request background location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Children's Privacy

The App is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
