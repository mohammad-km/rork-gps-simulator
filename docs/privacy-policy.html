Privacy Policy — GPS Simulator

Last updated: September 20, 2026

App name: GPS Simulator
Developer: Karam Mobile
Package name: com.rork.gpssimulator

This is the privacy policy for the Android app GPS Simulator ("the App"), published by Karam Mobile ("the developer"). The App is a developer testing tool that lets a user supply a mock (simulated) location to Android's location system, for software testing and development purposes. This policy explains what data the App accesses, what it sends over the network, what it stores on your device, and what it never does.

Summary

  • The developer runs no server and receives none of your data. The App has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no cookies.
  • The App reads your real location (only with your permission, and without background location access) to show it on the map, and it feeds the location you choose to Android's mock location provider so other apps on your device can see it.
  • The App connects directly from your device to third-party map tile providers and to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim service. They receive the visible map area, place-search text or selected coordinates, and your IP address.
  • Settings, saved places, routes, history, and the last position stay on your device. If Android device backup is turned on, Android may copy them to your Google account backup.

No Account, No Server of Ours

The App has no user accounts, and Karam Mobile does not operate any backend server. The developer does not receive or collect any of your personal data, your location, or the data stored in the App, and does not sell it. The App contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, does not use cookies or advertising identifiers, and does not read device identifiers such as your Android ID or IMEI. The only network connections the App makes go directly from your device to the third-party services described in "Map Tiles and Place Search" below. The App uses only open-source libraries (such as AndroidX, Kotlin, Ktor, and Koin), none of which is an analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting library.

Location Access

The App requests foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) to show your real current position on the in-app map, so you can pick a point to simulate or compare it against your simulated position, and to show live values in its GPS Diagnostics screen. The real position is read through Android's location providers: GPS, network, or, if "Use Google Play Location Services" is enabled (which it is by default), the fused provider that Google Play services supplies on your device. The App does not include any Google Play services SDK.

The App does not request background location permission, but the real-location listener may keep running while the app's process is alive and its screen is bound (for example while the app is open, even if temporarily not on screen), not only at the exact moment the screen is visible. It stops when the app's screen component is destroyed. Your real position itself is kept in memory only, and the App does not send it to the developer or to any server of its own. It can indirectly reach the map tile providers described below if you center the map on it.

Map Tiles and Place Search (Third-Party Servers)

To display the map, the App downloads map tiles from third-party map providers depending on the map type you choose: OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery, OpenTopoMap, or OpenStreetMap France (Humanitarian tiles). Loading a map tile sends the tile coordinates of the visible map area (which can include your real location if you center the map on it) and your IP address to that provider's servers, the same way any map app or web page with an embedded map would. Each request also carries a User-Agent that identifies the App ("GPSSimulator/1.0 (Android developer testing tool)"); the App sends no account identifiers, device identifiers, or cookies. All of these connections use HTTPS. Map tiles are kept in memory while the App runs and are not saved to your device's storage.

If you use the in-app place search, or select a point (on the map, from a search result, or by entering coordinates), the App sends the text you type in the search box (as you type) or the coordinates of the selected point to the OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoding service, which is operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation, in order to return a place name or address. These requests carry the same User-Agent. This happens when you search, select a point, or when the App restores your last simulated point at startup (if "Restore Last Test Location" is enabled, which it is by default) — not continuously and not in the background.

These providers are independent third parties with their own privacy practices; the App does not control what they log. No account identifiers, contact information, or other personal data beyond what this section describes is sent to them.

Mock Location Data

Coordinates you choose to simulate are used to feed Android's mock location provider (via Developer Options → "Select mock location app") on your own device. The App supplies them to the device's GPS and network location providers and, on Android 12 and later, to the fused provider, so other apps on your device that request location see the simulated location while you test them. While a simulated session is running, the App keeps supplying it from a foreground service that shows a persistent notification (which displays the simulated coordinates) until you stop the session. What other apps do with the simulated location is governed by their own privacy policies, not by this one.

This data stays on your device for the mock-location purpose described above and is never sent to the developer. The only time the App itself sends a chosen coordinate over the network is the place-name lookup described in the "Map Tiles and Place Search" section above, when applicable. If you choose to share, copy, or export a location or route, it goes to the app, clipboard, or file you pick; the App does not send it anywhere on its own.

Local Storage on Your Device

The App stores the following locally on your device only — never transmitted to the developer:

  • App settings and preferences (map style, units, etc.)
  • If "Remember Last Map Position" is enabled (on by default), the last map centre shown — which may be your real location if you last viewed the map there
  • The last point you simulated. It is saved whenever a simulated session runs, and if "Restore Last Test Location" is enabled (on by default) it is selected again when you reopen the App
  • The most recent simulated session's state, including its coordinates and place name, so a running session can resume if Android restarts the App's service. After you stop a session it is marked inactive but stays stored until your next session or until you delete the App's data
  • If "Save Location History" is enabled (on by default), a history of your simulated sessions (place name, address, coordinates, start time, and duration)
  • Your recent searches (the last 10 search queries you entered)
  • Your favourites and presets, your saved routes, and the geofences you create

This data never leaves your device via any developer-operated service, and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. In Settings you can turn off "Remember Last Map Position" and "Save Location History", clear the history with "Clear History", or remove everything with "Delete All Local App Data".

The last simulated point and the most recent session's state are stored regardless of these switches (including "Restore Last Test Location", which only controls whether the point is selected again when you reopen the App); "Delete All Local App Data" removes them.

Android backup: the App allows Android's standard app-data backup. If device backup is turned on (for example, backup to your Google account), Android may include this local data in your backup and restore it when you set up a new device. That backup is created and controlled by Android and Google under your account settings; Karam Mobile cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device's system settings.

Permissions Summary

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION; foreground only): to show your real position on the map and in GPS Diagnostics, as described above.
  • Mock location (ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION): declared so that the App can be chosen under Developer Options → "Select mock location app". Android lets the App supply mock locations only after you choose it there.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to download map tiles, to perform the place lookups described above, and to check whether a network is available (shown in GPS Diagnostics).
  • Foreground service and notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): to keep a simulated session running with the required "Mock location active" notification, which shows the simulated coordinates, and to let you stop the session from the notification.

The App does not request background location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or storage permissions. If you use Export or Import, the App reads or writes only the file you choose in Android's file picker. You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device's system settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is a developer tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
